Case study 01 · Product analytics

Product Pulse — Activation & Retention Analytics

An interactive Tableau analysis of the user funnel, from signup through activation, retention and paid conversion, to identify the highest-leverage product change to test first.

AnalyticsProduct strategyTableauExperiment design

About the data: a synthetic dataset of 6,000 users (Jan–Aug 2026), generated to practice product analytics. The findings are illustrative and don't describe a real product.

Overview: KPI row, monthly cohort retention for weeks 1–4, and 7-day activation by device and acquisition channel.

Deep dive: mobile trails desktop in all four channels (gaps of 13.6–16.8 points), plus template usage compared with week-4 retention.

Question

Where is the funnel leaking?

Only about half of users activate, and fewer than one in five are still active in week 4. Which funnel stage should the team prioritize?

Analysis

Cohort and segment analysis

Built a cohort retention heatmap and segmented activation by device and acquisition channel to test whether the drop-off is broad-based or concentrated.

Recommendation

Prioritize mobile onboarding

The mobile gap persists across every channel, pointing to onboarding friction rather than acquisition quality.

Mobile activates less than desktop

A 15.9-point gap overall, and mobile is lower in all four acquisition channels.

Template users retain better

Observational, not causal

12.4%

Week-4 retention for template users (n=1,924) vs. non-users (n=4,076). This is an association: more engaged users may simply be more likely to use templates.

Proposed, not yet run

Next step: streamlined mobile onboarding experiment

Proposed A/B test. Hypothesis: a shorter mobile onboarding flow closes the activation gap without degrading retention or monetization. Not yet run, and no results are claimed.

Case study 02 · Product design & build

My Board — Work Task & Account Management

Managing multiple enterprise accounts meant tasks, deadlines, follow-ups and call notes were fragmented across tools. Scoped, designed and built a mobile-first product that consolidates each account's workflow into a single view.

Product designAccount managementMobile-firstWeb app
Open live app GitHub

Work account organization

Each account gets its own icon, contact and color, with one-tap switching and a progress count on every chip.

Tracking & prioritization

To do, in progress and done states, plus priority flags, owners and due dates.

Progress metrics

Status tiles plus due-today, overdue and completion counts (e.g. 14/22) show where things stand.

Reminders & notifications

Reminders for overdue and upcoming tasks, timed to the due date, with optional pop-ups.

Personalization

Profile photo, role, English or Español, and dark or light appearance.

Mobile-first UI

Thumb-zone navigation, bottom sheets and large touch targets, optimized for on-the-go use.

Explored concept

AI meeting summarization

Scoped as a roadmap opportunity: turning call notes into summaries and follow-up tasks. Exploratory concept only, not deployed, with no time-savings claims.
